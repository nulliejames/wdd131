WDD 131 Project

Coastal Sierra Travel

A welcoming guide to the beauty, culture, and adventure of Sierra Leone.

1. Site name and purpose

Site Name

Coastal Sierra Travel

I chose this name because it reflects the country’s Atlantic coast, local travel experiences, and the warm welcome visitors can expect.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help people learn about Sierra Leone, discover top attractions, and plan trips with useful information about weather, culture, and local highlights.

2. Target audience and scenarios

  • Scenario 1: Where can I find the best places to visit, local foods to try, and practical tips for planning a memorable trip to Sierra Leone?
  • Scenario 2: What are Sierra Leone’s major destinations, and what should I know about the country’s culture and geography before I travel?
  • Scenario 3: How can my family quickly check local weather, compare destinations, and find helpful travel information while planning a vacation?

3. Naming conventions

  • project/siteplan.html — main project planning document
  • project/siteplan.css — stylesheet for the site plan design
  • images/ — shared image assets for the final website
  • styles/ — reusable site styling for other pages
  • scripts/ — JavaScript files for interactivity and date updates

4. Color scheme

#1EB53A Sierra Leone green · highlights
#FFFFFF Flag white · page surfaces
#0072C6 Flag blue · links and accents
#EAF6EC Pale green · soft panels
#EAF4FB Pale blue · cards and navigation

The green, white, and blue are drawn from Sierra Leone’s flag. Green highlights nature and destinations, blue is used for travel links and ocean accents, and white keeps the page bright and easy to read. Soft green and blue tints support the three flag colors without competing with them.

5. Typography

Headings

Poppins

Used for titles, section headings, and important labels.

Body Text

Source Sans 3

Used for paragraphs, lists, and general content readability.
